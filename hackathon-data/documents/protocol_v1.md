# Protocol STUDY-042 (Version 1.0)

**Title**: A Double-Blind, Randomized Phase III Study Evaluating Efficacy and Safety of Candesartan-X vs Placebo.
**Active Cuts**: Cut 1 through Cut 3
**Stopping Rule**: Concurrent ALT > 3x ULN and Total Bilirubin > 2x ULN (FDA Hy's Law criteria).

**Visit Window**: Target visit day +/- 3 calendar days.
