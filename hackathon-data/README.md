# STUDY-042 Clinical Trial Dataset

Synthetic Phase III diabetes-drug-vs-placebo trial.
- 241 subjects enrolled across 12 investigational sites (S01 to S12)
- 27,125 total records across 12 sequential surveillance cuts
- Protocol: STUDY-042 (v1.0 at baseline, v2.0 at Cut 4, v3.0 at Cut 8)
- CDISC SDTM domains: DM, LB, AE, EX, CM, VS, DS, MH, EG
- Longitudinal cuts: 1 to 12 with cut_available and corrected_at_cut tracking
